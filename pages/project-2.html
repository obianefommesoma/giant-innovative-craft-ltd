Apex Corporate Plaza

Location Abuja, Nigeria
Year 2026
Status Completed

The Challenge

Our client needed a modern, sustainable office space that maximized natural light while maintaining high energy efficiency. The project required precise structural engineering to support the glass facade.

Our Solution

We implemented a reinforced steel frame with specialized thermal glass. Our team managed everything from site preparation to the final interior finishing, ensuring the project was delivered on schedule.

Technical Details

  • Area: 12,500 Sq Ft
  • Floors: 12 Stories
  • Material: Steel & Glass
  • Sustainability: LEED Certified
