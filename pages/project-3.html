Titan Logistics Hub

LocationPort Harcourt
Load Cap500 Tons/m²
Timeline14 Months

Large Scale Logistics

This massive warehouse and distribution center required a specialized flat-floor system for high-reach forklifts. The structure had to be fireproof and capable of supporting heavy industrial machinery.

Engineering Strength

Using pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB), we achieved clear spans of 60 meters without internal columns, maximizing the storage volume for the client.

Industrial Specs

  • Roofing: Insulated Sandwich Panels
  • Flooring: Laser-Leveled Concrete
  • Security: 24/7 Automated Access
