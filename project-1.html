The Glass Pavilion

LocationLagos, Nigeria
TypeLuxury Villa
Size8,500 Sq Ft

The Vision

Designed for a private client, this pavilion-style home emphasizes open spaces and a seamless connection with the outdoors. The main challenge was implementing floor-to-ceiling glass walls while ensuring privacy and structural integrity.

The Result

A stunning three-story residence featuring a cantilevered balcony and an infinity pool. We used premium reinforced glass and sustainable cooling systems to manage the tropical climate.

Technical Details

  • Bedrooms: 6 Ensuite
  • Foundation: Deep Piling
  • Key Feature: Smart Home Integration
